Fellowship Program

Connect High School Students with Industry

Our Fellowship Program bridges the gap between FIRST Robotics teams and paid internship opportunities with local tech companies.

  • Paid internships for qualified students
  • Mentorship from industry professionals
  • Real-world project experience
  • College application portfolio building
  • Networking opportunities

Competitions

We support and participate in various robotics competitions throughout the year.

FIRST Robotics Competition (FRC)

High school students design and build robots to compete in alliance format field game.

VEX Robotics Competition

Middle and high school students compete with custom-built robots in challenging game scenarios.

BattleBots IQ

Combat robotics competition for students to design and battle their own robots.

Local Innovation Challenges

Community-driven competitions focusing on solving real-world problems with robotics.
