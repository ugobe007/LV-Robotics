Semi-Annual Hack-a-thon

Join innovators, students, and professionals in tackling real-world challenges twice a year. Build solutions, learn new skills, and connect with Las Vegas's robotics community.

Learn New Skills

Master cutting-edge robotics technologies, AI, and programming through hands-on challenges and mentorship from industry experts.

Solve Real Problems

Work on challenges that matter to local businesses. Create solutions with real-world impact and industry relevance.

Build Community

Connect with local engineers, entrepreneurs, and corporate sponsors. Build awareness and support for robotics in Las Vegas.

High School Students

College Students

The Future is Humanoid

Building Tomorrow's Robots Today

Humanoid robotics is moving from research labs into the real world. Our community explores the cutting edge of bipedal locomotion, dexterous manipulation, and embodied AI.

Whether you're fascinated by the mechanics of balance or the intelligence behind movement, there's a place for you here to learn, build, and experiment alongside fellow innovators.
