Community Impact

🎓 Student Scholarships

Thanks to community donors, we've provided over $50,000 in scholarships to help underserved students participate in robotics programs.

🏆 Equipment Donations

Community members have donated robotics kits worth over $100,000, enabling more teams to compete and learn.

👥 Volunteer Hours

Over 5,000 volunteer hours contributed by community members have helped us reach 2,000+ students annually.

🌟 Mentorship Connections

Hundreds of students have found mentors through community connections, gaining career guidance and inspiration.

🚀 Internship Opportunities

Community businesses have created over 100 internship positions for our students, launching their tech careers.

🎯 Free Programs

Community support enables us to offer free robotics workshops to schools and after-school programs in underserved neighborhoods.
