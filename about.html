Our Mission

LV Robotics is dedicated to fostering innovation, education, and collaboration in the Las Vegas robotics community. We bring together students, professionals, educators, and enthusiasts to explore, learn, and create the future of robotics and automation.

What We Do

Six ways we help people build, learn, and connect around robotics.

Workshops & Training

Hands-on workshops covering robotics fundamentals, programming, electronics, and advanced topics for all skill levels.

Competitions

Robotics challenges and competitions that push creativity, problem-solving, and technical skills to new heights.

Community Events

Regular meetups, demo days, and networking events connecting robotics enthusiasts across Las Vegas.

Fellowship Program

Mentorship and guidance for aspiring roboticists, providing pathways to careers in robotics and automation.

Partnerships

Collaborations with schools, universities, and companies to advance robotics education and innovation.

Projects & Labs

Access to maker spaces and resources for building, testing, and showcasing robotics projects.
