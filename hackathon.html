What is Our Hack-a-thon?

The LV Robotics Hack-a-thon is a semi-annual innovation event where high school students, college students, local entrepreneurs, and professional engineers come together to solve real-world robotics challenges presented by local businesses. It's not just about competition—it's about learning, collaboration, and building a stronger robotics community in Las Vegas.

Learn New Skills

Master cutting-edge robotics technologies, AI, programming languages, and hardware integration through hands-on challenges, alongside experienced engineers and real-time mentorship.

Solve Real Problems

Tackle challenges that matter to the Las Vegas business community. Your solutions could have real-world impact and help local companies innovate in robotics and automation.

Build Community

Connect with like-minded innovators, build lasting relationships, and help raise awareness and support for robotics in Las Vegas — bringing talent back to Vegas.

Who Can Participate?

Our hack-a-thon is open to all skill levels and backgrounds. Whether you're just starting out or an experienced professional, there's a place for you.

High School Students

Including robotics team members. A perfect opportunity to apply what you've learned and gain real-world experience — no prior hack-a-thon experience needed.

College Students

From all majors — engineering, computer science, business, design. Bring your unique perspective and build your portfolio.

Entrepreneurs

Explore new business opportunities, validate ideas, and connect with potential collaborators, talent, and industry leaders.

Professional Engineers

Share your expertise as a mentor, judge, or participant. Give back to the community while staying sharp and making a lasting impact.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need prior robotics experience?

No! We welcome all skill levels. Teams are encouraged to have a mix of experience levels, and mentors will be available throughout the event.

How much does it cost to participate?

Participation is FREE! All equipment, materials, meals, and snacks are provided by our generous sponsors.

Can I join solo or do I need a team?

Both! You can register as a solo participant and we'll match you with a team, or you can register as a pre-formed team of 3-5 people.

What should I bring?

Bring your laptop, charger, and enthusiasm! We provide all robotics hardware, equipment, and materials. Dress comfortably for a hands-on working environment.

When is the next hack-a-thon?

Our next event is planned for Spring 2026. Sign up for our newsletter or follow us on social media to get notified when registration opens!

How are high school students supervised?

High school participants must have parental consent. The event is fully supervised with multiple adult mentors, organizers, and volunteers present at all times during the event.
