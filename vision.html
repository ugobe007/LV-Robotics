The Opportunity

Las Vegas Is Already the World's Stage

The global demand exists. The dedicated infrastructure is missing. Vision Vegas 2040 builds the foundation that turns Las Vegas into the undisputed global hub for robot staging, activation, and deployment.

01 / Global Demand

The World Comes Here

Every major robotics company travels to Las Vegas for CES and MINExpo to unveil their technology. The city is already the undisputed global stage for physical AI.

02 / The White Space

No Infrastructure Exists

There is zero dedicated infrastructure to support them. Companies are forced to unpack, test, and repair million-dollar robots in hotel rooms and loading docks.

03 / Local Foundation

600+ Ready Builders

A growing ecosystem already calls Las Vegas home. Over 600 engineers and founders are ready to anchor the ecosystem today.

The Vision

The AI Robot Flywheel

Each pillar feeds the next. Education creates the talent that powers the Ecosystem. The Ecosystem funds the infrastructure that enables Services. Services generate the revenue and data that flow back to fund Education — compounding with every cycle.

Pillar 01

Education

Training the workforce — certified operators, technicians, and engineers ready for the robot economy.

Pillar 02

Ecosystem

Building Automation Alley — the physical campus and venture fund investing in embodied AI startups.

Pillar 03

Services

Generating revenue through StageGate, Certifications, Forge, and Galaxy Labs for global robot companies.

The Three Pillars

How We Build It

Education — The Workforce the Robot Economy Requires

Four targeted education pathways, in partnership with UNLV and CSN, that train students for the specific skills the robot industry needs — so Nevada's top engineering talent stays home to build the future.

01

Robot Skills

Service, maintenance, and optimization training for robot operators.

02

Operator Certification

Classified training for humans working safely alongside robots.

03

Career Connections

Matching students with robot companies for jobs and internships.

04

Robot Engineering

Firmware, motor systems, sensors, and authoring tools for builders.

Ecosystem — Automation Alley & The Alley Fund

Automation Alley

The physical campus — dedicated demo floors, testing bays, and workspace anchoring a dense community of founders, researchers, and global companies.

The $100M Alley Fund

Early-stage syndicates and mid-stage growth capital backing physical-demo, hardware-first founders — with capital, access, and community no traditional VC can replicate.

Accelerator & Advisors

Cohort programs across Build, Scale, and Deploy, plus 50+ advisors — founders, CTOs, and operators from leading robot companies.
